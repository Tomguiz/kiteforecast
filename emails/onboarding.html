⭐  Your spot, your wind

 

A forecast that says “18 knots” is useless if the wind is blowing straight offshore at your beach. So you tell KiteForecast which directions actually work where you ride, and it only calls a session good when the wind is both strong enough and coming from the right way.

Not sure which directions? Whatever the locals say — and if you spot something wrong on a spot, you can suggest a fix and earn free premium for it.

🔔  Get told, don't go looking

 

This is the part that changes how you use the app. Pick a promising session on the forecast and set an alert — KiteForecast emails you as it approaches, so a good day never quietly passes while you weren't looking.

You choose the timing. The 1 hour one is the good stuff: it carries the live measured wind, not a forecast made this morning.

🌬  What the wind is doing right now

 

Where there's a weather mast near your spot, the spot page shows the measured wind rather than a model's guess — so you can decide whether to load the car on facts. Coverage is good along the Dutch and Zeeland coast and thinner elsewhere; where there's no mast nearby, it simply shows nothing rather than making something up.

And on your spots list, anywhere blowing 15 kn+ from a direction that works lights up: 🔥 24 kn

👥  Nobody likes riding alone

 

Add the people you ride with and you'll see who's already committed to a session — their initial shows up on the spot the moment they say they're going. Half the reason a good forecast goes to waste is nobody wanting to drive out on their own.
