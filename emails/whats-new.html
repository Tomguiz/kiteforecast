Some spot alerts and weekly digests were being skipped — silently. Windy days came and went without an email. We found it, fixed it, and added tests so it can't come back quietly.

Spot pages now show what the nearest weather mast is measuring right now — not what a model predicted this morning. Your 1 hour reminder carries it too, so you know before you load the car.

📍 GOOD SESSIONS NEAR HOME

 

Set a home location and the weekly digest adds a second section: the best spots near you that you haven't favourited. It spreads them out — no point listing four beaches on the same stretch of coast sharing one forecast — and it won't send you 100 km for a two-hour window.

👥 WHO'S ACTUALLY GOING

 

When a mate confirms a session, their initial appears on that spot in your list. And the app now tells you plainly which friends actually receive your alerts — if someone turned notifications off, you'll see it instead of wondering.
